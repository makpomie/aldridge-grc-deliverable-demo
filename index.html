What this demonstrates

  • — A shared context artefact that every deliverable reads from and writes back to, so figures reconcile across documents instead of drifting.
  • — Evidence, effort, cost and dependency tracking attached to every finding, not just a maturity rating asserted without a basis.
  • — A capacity check that surfaces a remediation plan is not deliverable as assigned, before anyone approves it.
  • — An expert critique pass that scored the first draft against practitioner standards, then drove a second, more rigorous build.
